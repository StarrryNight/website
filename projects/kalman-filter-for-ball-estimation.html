Kalman Filter for Ball Estimation

Estimating ball position and velocity from noisy vision data on a RoboCup SSL team.

The problem

Overhead cameras report the ball's position at a fixed rate, but the readings are noisy and drop out when the ball is occluded. Downstream logic needs a smooth estimate of both position and velocity.

We model the ball with state xk​=[px​,py​,vx​,vy​]T and assume constant velocity between frames:

xk​=Fxk−1​+wk−1​,w∼N(0,Q)

where the transition matrix is

F=​1000​0100​Δt010​0Δt01​​

The update

Each frame gives a measurement zk​=Hxk​+vk​ with v∼N(0,R) . The gain is

Kk​=Pk∣k−1​HT(HPk∣k−1​HT+R)−1

and the corrected estimate is x^k∣k​=x^k∣k−1​+Kk​(zk​−Hx^k∣k−1​) .

def predict(x, P, F, Q):
    x = F @ x
    P = F @ P @ F.T + Q
    return x, P

Result

Blocking accuracy improved by 25%, and the estimate stayed usable through occlusions of up to four frames.
